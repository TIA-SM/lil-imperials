Official Contest Rules & Guidelines

  • Eligibility: Open to young chefs aged 4 to 15. Parent or legal guardian consent and supervision are required for all submissions.
  • Submission Window: Entries are accepted starting August 1 through August 18. Late submissions will not be eligible for voting or prizes.
  • Video Specifications: Videos must be between 3 to 5 minutes in length, formatted as MP4 or MOV, featuring the child preparing and presenting a recipe using Saladmaster cookware.
  • Parental Guidance: Parents may assist with stove operation, oven work, or heavy lifting for safety, but the child chef must take the primary active role in the video.
  • Voting Rules: Voting is limited to one vote per device or network address to ensure a fair and equitable contest.
  • Sharing: Share your entry to others to gather more votes.
  • Winner Announcement: Grand prize and consolation winners will be officially announced during our August Social Hour event.
